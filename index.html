Community Voting

Your voice shapes the directory

Logged-in members can vote on any business. Recommendations are visible on listings and help other shoppers decide with confidence.

👍 Recommend

Had a great experience? Recommend the business. Once a business reaches 5 recommendations from our community, they earn the Community-Recommended badge on their listing.

👎 Not Great

Had a disappointing experience? Your vote is recorded privately — it's never shown publicly on a business's listing, but it factors into whether that business earns the Community-Recommended badge.
