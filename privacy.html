Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Loonie List is operated by FlyFinder, an app studio based in Kingston, Ontario, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Loonie List iPhone app and website collect, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account:

  • Your name, email address, and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Whether you joined as a shopper or a business owner.

If you list a business:

  • Business name, category, description, city, province, and postal code.
  • Optional contact details you choose to add: website, phone number, and business email.
  • Whether you have a physical store, offer online ordering, and where you ship.
  • Your logo, product photos, and product names, descriptions, and prices.
  • We use your postal code to find the approximate map location of your business so shoppers can sort by distance.

When you use the app:

  • Your thumbs-up and thumbs-down votes on businesses, and the businesses you save as favourites.
  • Safety reports you submit, including the business or person named, the city and province, and your description.
  • If you buy the optional "Support Loonie List" in-app purchase, a record of that purchase (see section 4).

Location: If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location (to about one kilometre) to sort businesses by distance. This happens on your device. Your location is not sent to our servers or stored with your account. You can turn location off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Loonie List does not use advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and run your account and let you sign in and reset your password.
  • To show business listings, search results, votes, and favourites.
  • To review new business listings before they appear publicly, and to review safety reports.
  • To process the optional in-app purchase.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

3. What Is Public

  • Business listings: Everything in a business profile, including its photos, products, and any contact details you add, is shown publicly in the app.
  • Votes: The number of thumbs-up votes on a business is public. Your own votes are not shown with your name.
  • Safety reports: Reports are anonymous by default. An anonymous report does not show your identity to the business or the public. We still keep a link between the report and your account so we can review it and prevent abuse.

4. In-App Purchases

Loonie List is free. It offers one optional in-app purchase, "Support Loonie List", which is a one-time way to support the app. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases. When you open the app, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, and when you are signed in, your Loonie List account ID, so it can link purchases to your account. If you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives the purchase details from Apple, such as the product, price, date, and country. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or location from us.

5. Service Providers

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the companies that run parts of the app for us:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system, and photo storage, and sends account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset.
  • RevenueCat manages in-app purchases (see section 4).
  • Apple processes App Store purchases under its own privacy policy.

These providers may store data outside Canada, including in the United States. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

6. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app: open Profile, then tap Delete Account and confirm. This immediately and permanently removes your account, your profile, your votes, your favourites, and any business listings and products you created.

Safety reports you submitted may be kept after your account is deleted so we can keep protecting the community. Records of in-app purchases are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for financial record keeping. If you cannot access the app, email hello@loonielist.app from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

7. Your Rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial laws, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to its use. Email hello@loonielist.app and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Loonie List is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal information, we will delete it.

9. Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules limit each account to editing only its own information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will also let you know in the app.
